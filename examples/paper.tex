\section{Related work}
Attention-only architectures \citep{vaswani2017attention} and denoising diffusion
\citep{ho2020denoising} are now standard building blocks. Retrieval-augmented diffusion
transformers extend both to long-horizon planning \citep{zhang2024retrieval}, and the
denoising objective has been analysed through score matching \citep{song2020denoising}.
We train all models with Adam \citep{kingma2011adam}.
